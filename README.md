# Hi, I'm Kübra 👋

🌐 **Portfolio:** [kubrato.github.io](https://kubrato.github.io) · 📄 [CV (PDF)](https://kubrato.github.io/cv.pdf)

I work where **technology, processes and the humanities** meet. I'm an MSc student in Digital Humanities and Digital Knowledge at the University of Bologna, with a background in computer education, almost two years as a Business Analyst in R&D, and hands-on front-end development.

Right now I'm exploring how AI agents can support cultural heritage work, especially the digitisation of archives and library collections, and how formalising processes and requirements can make AI systems more reliable.

## 🔭 What I'm working on
- 🎓 **MSc thesis:** *Towards an agentic digitisation workflow*, testing whether AI agents can act as quality-assurance supervisors in the Zeri auction catalogues project (~1,600 catalogues, 200,000 images). Supervised by Prof. Paolo Bonora.
- 🕸️ **Network analysis project:** mining collaboration networks from the BPI Challenge 2012 event log (~13,000 loan applications) to compare approved and declined cases, using PM4Py, NetworkX and Gephi.
- 🌱 **Interests:** AI agents in cultural heritage · human-in-the-loop quality assurance · BPMN and process mining · evaluating LLM decisions against human ones · social network analysis
